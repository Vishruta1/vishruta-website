ChatuR : SMART HUMAN DETECTION SYSTEM

Intelligent human detection and zone monitoring system with real-time alerts, multi-camera support and low-light accuracy.

OBJECTIVE

To detect human presence in defined zones or geofenced areas in real-time with high accuracy, even in challenging environments, and trigger instant alerts and logging.

Platform Overview

INTELLIGENT PERIMETER & SAFETY ARCHITECTURE

Purpose-built computer vision engine designed to safeguard personnel, detect unauthorized intrusions, and monitor high-risk industrial zones 24/7.

01 / AI CORE

AI Detection Engine

Advanced neural vision model trained to identify human presence across diverse postures, orientations, personal protective equipment (PPE), and movement speeds.

02 / REAL-TIME STREAMING

Multi-Camera Ingestion

Connects directly to existing IP cameras and NVR/DVRs via RTSP / ONVIF, delivering sub-second edge inference at 25–30 FPS with ultra-low hardware overhead.

03 / SUPERVISION

Centralized Dashboard

Unified operations console providing live multi-zone status, instant audio-visual intrusion alarms, video evidence review, and downloadable audit logs.

04 / SCALABILITY

Scalable Architecture

From a single edge IPC monitoring a robotic workcell to multi-server plant deployments coordinating hundreds of cameras across widespread facilities.

Capabilities

KEY FEATURES OF ChatuR

Engineered for safety managers, plant security officers, and industrial automation leads.

🎯

Real-Time Human Detection

Continuous real-time verification of human presence with sub-second response times, triggering instant perimeter alerts the moment a boundary is breached.

📐

Customizable Geofenced Zones

Interactive polygonal zone configuration allows administrators to map complex machinery perimeters, restricted hazard cages, and directional tripwires with ease.

🌙

Low-Light & Thermal Accuracy

Seamlessly integrates with both visible spectrum and infrared/thermal optical cameras, maintaining dependable tracking in total darkness, smoke, or shadow shifts.

🛡️

Intelligent False Alarm Suppression

AI filters out non-human motion including stray animals, automated guided vehicles (AGVs), moving machinery parts, foliage, and rain ripples.

📋

Automated Incident Logging

Every security or safety incident automatically archives timestamped high-resolution snapshot frames and video clips for OSHA, ISO, and regulatory compliance.

⚡

PLC Interlock & Relay Output

Direct hardware relay triggers, Modbus, MQTT, or dry contact outputs to instantly halt robotic cells, sound plant hooters, or flash emergency strobes upon breach.

Tested In Rigorous Industrial Scenarios

SAMPLE INPUT CONDITIONS HANDLED

Low Light & Night Scenes

Maintains high detection accuracy even in poorly illuminated storage bays and off-shift plant facilities.

Infrared / Thermal Imaging

Direct integration with FLIR and thermal optical sensors for total darkness or smoke conditions.

Occlusion & Obstacles

Tracks persons partially hidden behind pallets, structural pillars, machinery, or moving carts.

Shadows & Light Shift

Dynamic background modeling ensures moving machinery shadows are not classified as intruders.

Crowded Scenes

Distinguishes individual human entities in high-density shift-change doorways and canteen corridors.

Rain / Outdoor Weather

Weather-robust filters eliminate false triggers caused by heavy rain, fog, or flying insects.

Partial Visibility & Angles

Identifies persons from steep overhead angles, partially cropped camera views, or crouched positions.
