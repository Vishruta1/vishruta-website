VarNa : SMART CHARACTER RECOGNITION SYSTEM

AI-powered system for accurate text recognition from images under challenging conditions with minimal manual input.

OBJECTIVE

To enable accurate, reliable, and automated text recognition from images—even under challenging conditions such as incomplete characters, shading, smudges, and major visual defects—through a simple user interaction (just drawing a box).

Platform Overview

CORE CAPABILITIES & ARCHITECTURE

VarNa brings state-of-the-art computer vision to industrial inspection, eliminating complex model training and delivering sub-second character recognition directly on the line.

01 / ACCURACY

High Accuracy

Trained specifically on industrial typography, dot matrix stamping, metal embossing, and engraved serials, achieving reliable reading where standard OCR fails.

02 / INTELLIGENCE

AI & ML Powered

Deep learning vision models dynamically compensate for uneven lighting, shadows, surface glare, metal grain, and background distortion without manual threshold tuning.

03 / PERFORMANCE

Fast & Stable

Optimized inference engine running at sub-second speeds on industrial PCs and edge devices, easily matching continuous high-speed production and sorting lines.

04 / SIMPLICITY

Easy Integration

Zero deep learning expertise needed by operators. Integrates directly with industrial cameras, PLCs, SCADA, MES, and ERP systems via standard industrial protocols.

Operational Workflow

HOW VarNa RECOGNITION WORKS

Seamless 5-stage automated optical character recognition pipeline designed for minimal setup and maximum line efficiency.

1. Image Acquisition
Camera captures product surface on conveyor
➔
2. Draw Target Box
Operator draws region of interest with zero coding
➔
3. Neural Extraction
AI isolates characters from noise & shadows
➔
4. Auto Verification
Validates against ERP / barcode master database
➔
5. Pass/Fail Signal
Instant trigger sent to PLC & local audit log
Tested on Extreme Production Lines
