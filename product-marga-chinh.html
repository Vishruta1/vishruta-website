STAGE 01

Raw Material / Supplier

Raw materials are scanned via RFID tags and assigned a cryptographic genesis batch record. Origin, farm certificates, supplier QA and chemical purity metrics are verified upon dock receiving.

STAGE 02

Manufacturing Floor

Shop floor IoT sensors capture real-time production parameters, mixing temperatures, operator badges, and machine run cycles, logging all metrics securely to the cloud.

STAGE 03

Packaging & Serialization

Automated high-speed printing links unique 2D QR codes and encoded RFID tags onto primary packaging, case cartons, and shipping pallets with parent-child aggregation.

STAGE 04

Warehouse Management

UHF RFID gates log whole-pallet inbound and outbound transfers automatically without opening boxes, eliminating inventory discrepancies and manual counting errors.

STAGE 05

Distribution & Transit

Cellular GPS telematics track carrier routes, dispatch schedules, transit shocks, and temperature excursions in cold chain transit with real-time exception notifications.

STAGE 06

Retail Store & Consumer

Consumers scan the on-pack QR code using any smartphone to view provenance, freshness certificates, and anti-counterfeiting authenticity with zero app downloads.

Digital Pipeline

HOW IT WORKS

1. Scan RFID / QR Code:
Scanned automatically at each conveyor, dock door, or handheld checkpoint.
2. Real-Time Data Capture:
Operator ID, timestamp, machine telemetry, and sensor parameters are appended.
3. Secure Cloud Persistence:
Tamper-proof storage with ISO 27001 encrypted data handling and zero latency.
4. Analytics & Alerts Generated:
Automated deviation alerts, expiry warnings, and recall scoping executed instantly.
5. Actionable Insights on Dashboard:
Visual dashboards empower plant managers, logistics officers, and brand inspectors.
