Key Objective:

  • Prevent Unauthorized Usage (blocks specific UI elements or buttons)

VarNa VISION AI
Smart Character Recognition System

AI-powered system for accurate text recognition from images under challenging conditions with minimal manual input.

Key Objective:

To enable accurate, reliable, and automated text recognition from images—even under challenging conditions such as incomplete characters, shading, smudges, and major visual defects—through a simple user interaction (just drawing a box).

  • Improve accuracy under poor conditions (metal embossing, engraving, arced)
  • Reduce dependency on perfect lighting and image quality
  • Detects special characters, logos, and variable fonts via ML

ChatuR DETECTION AI
Smart Human Detection System

Intelligent human detection and zone monitoring system with real-time alerts, multi-camera support and low-light accuracy.

Key Objective:

To detect human presence in defined zones or geofenced areas in real-time with high accuracy, even in challenging environments, and trigger instant alerts and logging.

  • Real-Time Human Detection & instant alerts on unauthorized entry
  • Low-Light & Infrared / Thermal sensor support
  • False Alarm Reduction filtering out animals, shadows & objects
